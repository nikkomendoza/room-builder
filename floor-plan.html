Instructions

Click a catalog item to add furniture, or define your own name/width/depth above the catalog and click Add Custom Item.
Click any item (furniture, wall, door, window) to select it.
Drag a selected furniture or wall to move it.
Press R or the rotate button to turn furniture/walls 90°.
Press Delete/Backspace or the × button to remove the selection.
Drag a door or window along its wall to slide it.
Select an interior wall first, then click Add Door/Window to place it on that wall (otherwise it's added to the room's top wall).
Edit Width/Depth to resize the room (1″ steps).
